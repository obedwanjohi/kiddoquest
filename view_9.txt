1: # Kid UI Refactor: The Mission Flow
2: 
3: This plan outlines the major refactor needed to switch the Kid UI from the old "Lesson/Quiz" architecture to the new "Mission" architecture. 
4: 
5: ## Background
6: Previously, the child played a `Lesson` and then took a `Quiz`. We recently introduced the `Mission` model as the bridge between the curriculum and the game. Now, the child plays a `Mission` (which has a video, narration, and an assigned `QuestionBank`).
