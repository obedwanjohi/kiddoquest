KiddoQuest CBC Curriculum Play Group – Christian Religious Education (CRE)
Level
Play Group (Ages 3–4)
Overall Goal
Help children discover that God loves them, appreciate themselves and others, care for God's creation, develop good behavior, and enjoy simple Bible stories through fun and interactive learning.
