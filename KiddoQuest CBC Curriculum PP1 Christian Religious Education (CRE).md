Content Strategy
Each mission should:
Teach one clear Christian concept or value.
Use colourful 2D illustrations and relatable Kenyan contexts.
Include 8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
Present 5 random questions per play session.
Reinforce learning through different question types (multiple choice, matching, sequencing, true/false, drag & sort, and simple scenarios).
Focus on understanding and applying Christian values rather than memorizing facts.
