### ☀️ MISSION 1: `PP1-CRE-1.1.1` — God Made the Sun, Moon & Stars

#### STEP 1: Mission Metadata & Outcomes
- **Adventure World:** Creation & God's World (Strand 1.0)
- **Sub-Strand:** 1.1 God Created Everything
- **Admin Title:** God Made the Sun, Moon & Stars
- **Kid Display Title:** Heavenly Lights Creation! ☀️🌙
- **Target Skill:** Appreciate God as the Creator of the sun, moon, and stars, and practice morning/night gratitude.
- **Session Rules:** Draw 5 random questions per session from the 20-question pool.

#### STEP 2: The 20-Question Bank Pool

##### 🅰️ Group A: `multiple_choice` Mechanics (Questions 1 to 8)
1. **Q1:** Who created the glowing sun to give us day time light?
   - *Options:* God Almighty 🙏 ✅, Human Builders, Nobody
2. **Q2:** Which heavenly light did God place in the night sky?
   - *Options:* Moon & Stars 🌙⭐ ✅, Electric Lamp, Plastic Toy
3. **Q3:** Why did God make the warm sun for us?
   - *Options:* To give light & warmth ☀️ ✅, To make it dark, To make us cold
4. **Q4:** What should we say to God when we see the beautiful sky?
   - *Options:* "Thank You God!" 🙏 ✅, Nothing, "I am angry"
5. **Q5:** Tap the creation in the sky that God made for daytime!
   - *Options:* Sun ☀️ ✅, Car 🚗, Table 🪑
6. **Q6:** Who created the twinkling stars that shine at bedtime?
   - *Options:* God ✅, A Painter, A Pilot
7. **Q7:** Which book tells us that God created the heavens and the earth?
   - *Options:* The Holy Bible 📖 ✅, A Comic Book, A Newspaper
8. **Q8:** Tap the smiling sun that God created!
   - *Options:* Sun ☀️ ✅, Shoe 👟, Pencil ✏️

##### 🗣️ Group B: `speak_repeat` Mechanics (Questions 9 to 14)
9. **Q9:** Say out loud clearly: *"GOD MADE THE SUN!"*
   - *Target Word:* `God Made The Sun`
10. **Q10:** Say out loud: *"THANK YOU GOD FOR DAYLIGHT!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Thank You God For Daylight`

    - *Target Word:* `God Is Creator`

    - *Target Word:* `God Loves The World`
13. **Q13:** Say out loud: *"GOD MADE THE STARS!"*
    - *Target Word:* `God Made The Stars`
14. **Q14:** Say out loud: *"GLORY TO GOD!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Glory To God`

##### 🧩 Group C: `matching` Mechanics (Questions 15 to 20)
15. **Q15:** Match God's creations to time of day!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun ☀️` ↔ `Day Time`
    - *Pair 2:* `Moon & Stars 🌙⭐` ↔ `Night Time`
    - *Pair 3:* `Clouds ☁️` ↔ `Blue Sky`
16. **Q16:** Match prayers to God's gifts!
    - *Pair 1:* `Day Light` ↔ `Morning Thanksgiving Prayer`
    - *Pair 2:* `Bed Time` ↔ `Night Guardian Prayer`
    - *Pair 3:* `Nature` ↔ `Creation Care Prayer`
17. **Q17:** Match sky creations!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun` ↔ `Light & Warmth`
    - *Pair 2:* `Rain` ↔ `Water for Plants`
    - *Pair 3:* `Stars` ↔ `Night Sparkle`
18. **Q18:** Match actions of gratitude!
    - *Pair 1:* `Seeing Sun` ↔ `Folding Hands 🙏`
    - *Pair 2:* `Hearing Birds` ↔ `Singing Praise 🎵`
    - *Pair 3:* `Family` ↔ `Saying I Love You ❤️`
19. **Q19:** Match Bible verse phrases!
    - *Pair 1:* `God Created` ↔ `The Heavens`
    - *Pair 2:* `God Saw` ↔ `It Was Good`
    - *Pair 3:* `Praise` ↔ `The Lord`
20. **Q20:** Match creation symbols!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun` ↔ `☀️`
    - *Pair 2:* `Moon` ↔ `🌙`
    - *Pair 3:* `Star` ↔ `⭐`
