### 🐱 MISSION 1: `PP2-ENG-1.1.1` — The -AT Word Family Safari (Cat, Mat, Rat, Hat, Bat)

#### STEP 1: Mission Metadata & Outcomes
- **Adventure World:** Advanced Phonics & CVC Word Building (Strand 1.0)
- **Sub-Strand:** 1.1 CVC Word Families
- **Admin Title:** The -AT Word Family Safari
- **Kid Display Title:** The -AT Family Quest! 🐱
- **Target Skill:** Read, blend, and spell -AT family words (Cat, Mat, Rat, Hat, Bat).
- **Session Rules:** Draw 6 random questions per session from the 20-question pool.

#### STEP 2: The 20-Question Bank Pool

##### 🅰️ Group A: `multiple_choice` Mechanics (Questions 1 to 8)
1. **Q1:** Blend the sounds: /c/ /a/ /t/. What word does it make?
   - *Options:* CAT 🐱 ✅, DOG 🐶, SUN ☀️
2. **Q2:** Which picture matches the word **HAT**?
   - *Options:* Red Hat 🧢 ✅, Mat 🦶, Bat 🦇
3. **Q3:** Which letter completes the word: **_ AT** for the sleeping cat?
   - *Options:* `C` ✅, `B`, `M`
4. **Q4:** Which word rhymes with **CAT**?
   - *Options:* MAT 🦶 ✅, DOG 🐶, CUP 🥛
5. **Q5:** Tap the picture of a flying **BAT**!
   - *Options:* Bat 🦇 ✅, Rat 🐁, Hat 🧢
6. **Q6:** Which picture is a door **MAT**?
   - *Options:* Mat 🦶 ✅, Cat 🐱, Bat 🦇
7. **Q7:** Blend: /r/ /a/ /t/. Which animal is it?
   - *Options:* RAT 🐁 ✅, CAT 🐱, PIG 🐷
8. **Q8:** Tap the correct spelling for **CAT**!
   - *Options:* `CAT` ✅, `COT`, `CUT`

##### 🗣️ Group B: `speak_repeat` Mechanics (Questions 9 to 14)
9. **Q9:** Say out loud clearly: */c/ /a/ /t/ CAT!*
   - *Target Word:* `Cat`
10. **Q10:** Say out loud: *"THE CAT SAT ON THE MAT!"*
    - *Target Word:* `The Cat Sat On The Mat`
11. **Q11:** Say out loud: */h/ /a/ /t/ HAT!*
    - *Target Word:* `Hat`
12. **Q12:** Say out loud: */m/ /a/ /t/ MAT!*
    - *Target Word:* `Mat`
13. **Q13:** Say out loud: *"BAT AND RAT!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Bat And Rat`
14. **Q14:** Say out loud: *"-AT WORD FAMILY!"*
    - *Target Word:* `At Word Family`

##### 🧩 Group C: `matching` Mechanics (Questions 15 to 20)
15. **Q15:** Match CVC words to pictures!
    - *Pair 1:* `CAT` ↔ `🐱`
    - *Pair 2:* `HAT` ↔ `🧢`
    - *Pair 3:* `MAT` ↔ `🦶`
16. **Q16:** Match beginning sounds to -AT!
    - *Pair 1:* `/c/` + `-AT` ↔ `CAT`
    - *Pair 2:* `/h/` + `-AT` ↔ `HAT`
    - *Pair 3:* `/m/` + `-AT` ↔ `MAT`
17. **Q17:** Match rhyming words!
    - *Pair 1:* `CAT` ↔ `HAT`
    - *Pair 2:* `MAT` ↔ `RAT`
    - *Pair 3:* `BAT` ↔ `SAT`
18. **Q18:** Match uppercase CVC to lowercase CVC!
    - *Pair 1:* `CAT` ↔ `cat`
    - *Pair 2:* `HAT` ↔ `hat`
    - *Pair 3:* `MAT` ↔ `mat`
19. **Q19:** Match missing letters!
    - *Pair 1:* `C _ T` ↔ `A`
    - *Pair 2:* `H _ T` ↔ `A`
    - *Pair 3:* `M _ T` ↔ `A`
20. **Q20:** Match picture sentences!
    - *Pair 1:* `Cat on Mat` ↔ `🐱 🦶`
    - *Pair 2:* `Rat in Hat` ↔ `🐁 🧢`
    - *Pair 3:* `Bat on Tree` ↔ `🦇 🌳`
