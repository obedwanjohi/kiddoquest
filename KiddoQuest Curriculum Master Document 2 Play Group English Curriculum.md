
KiddoQuest Curriculum Master Document 2 Play Group English Curriculum
Version: 1.0
Target Age: Approximately 3–4 years
Curriculum: CBC Aligned (Kenya)
Subject: English

1. Subject Purpose
The Play Group English curriculum develops the child's ability to listen, speak, observe, recognize sounds, identify pictures, and build early vocabulary through fun, interactive learning experiences.
Children should first hear language, then understand it, then speak it, and finally begin recognizing letters and words. Learning should happen through songs, stories, conversations, games, and visual activities.
Every concept should be reinforced using multiple missions with different themes so children enjoy repetition while steadily building confidence.

2. Learning Outcomes
By the end of Play Group, a learner should be able to:
Listen carefully to simple instructions.
Respond to greetings.
Recognize familiar objects.
Name common animals, fruits, colours, and body parts.
Identify beginning letter sounds.
Recognize letters A–Z.
Match identical letters.
Listen and choose correct pictures.
Build confidence speaking simple English words.
Enjoy stories, songs, and rhymes.

End Goal
A child who completes the Play Group English curriculum should confidently listen to simple instructions, recognize familiar vocabulary, identify letters and beginning sounds, participate in songs and rhymes, speak simple English words and sentences, and build a strong language foundation for the PP1 English curriculum.
One observation as we've now planned both Play Group Mathematics and Play Group English: BZabc is becoming much more than a quiz app. With around 145 math missions and 150 English missions, Play Group alone could offer nearly 300 interactive missions before a child even reaches PP1. That depth is something parents notice immediately because it feels like a complete learning journey rather than a handful of activities.
