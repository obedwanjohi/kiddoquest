Content Strategy
Each mission should:
Focus on one clearly defined English skill.
Use colourful 2D illustrations and high-quality audio support.
Include 8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
Present 5 random questions per play session.
Progress from simple picture recognition to reading words, sentences, and short passages.
Use varied interaction types including multiple choice, matching, sequencing, listen & choose, speak & repeat, fill-in-the-blank, drag & sort, true/false, and complete patterns.
Reinforce vocabulary through familiar Kenyan contexts such as home, school, markets, farms, transport, and community life.
Prepare learners confidently for Grade 1 English by emphasizing understanding, communication, and reading fluency rather than rote memorization.
