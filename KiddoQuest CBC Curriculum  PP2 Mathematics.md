Content Strategy
Each mission should:
Teach one clearly defined mathematical skill.
Use colourful 2D illustrations with Kenyan-friendly examples.
Include 8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
Present 5 random questions per play session.
Increase difficulty gradually across the strand.
Use a variety of interaction types including multiple choice, matching, sequencing, drag & sort, true/false, count objects, complete patterns, listen & choose, and fill-in-the-blank where appropriate.
Reinforce the same concept through different themes such as fruits, animals, toys, classrooms, farms, markets, transport, and everyday life.
