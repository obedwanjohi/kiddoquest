### 🛡️ MISSION 1: `PP2-CRE-1.1.1` — Little David & Courageous Faith

#### STEP 1: Mission Metadata & Outcomes
- **Adventure World:** Creation & The Bible Realm (Strand 1.0)
- **Sub-Strand:** 1.1 Bible Story Adventures
- **Admin Title:** Little David & Courageous Faith
- **Kid Display Title:** Brave David & Goliath! 🛡️
- **Target Skill:** Understand the story of David, recognize that God gives us courage to do good, and trust God in scary situations.
- **Session Rules:** Draw 6 random questions per session from the 20-question pool.

#### STEP 2: The 20-Question Bank Pool

##### 🅰️ Group A: `multiple_choice` Mechanics (Questions 1 to 8)
1. **Q1:** Who gave young David courage when he faced Goliath?
   - *Options:* God Almighty 🙏 ✅, King Saul, Himself
2. **Q2:** What was little David's job in the fields before becoming king?
   - *Options:* A Shepherd caring for sheep 🐑 ✅, A Builder, A Fisherman
3. **Q3:** Which tool did David use with faith in God?
   - *Options:* A Sling & Smooth Stones 🪨 ✅, A Heavy Sword, A Bow
4. **Q4:** What lesson does David teach us when we feel scared?
   - *Options:* Trust in God for help 🙏 ✅, Run away, Cry loud
5. **Q5:** Tap the picture of brave shepherd David!
   - *Options:* David with Shepherd Crook 🐑 ✅, King on Throne, Goliath
6. **Q6:** Which book in the Bible tells us the story of David?
   - *Options:* The Old Testament 📖 ✅, A Comic, A Storybook
7. **Q7:** How did David protect his sheep from wild animals?
   - *Options:* God helped him be brave 🛡️ ✅, He ran away, He hid
8. **Q8:** Tap the heart that shows courage and faith in God!
   - *Options:* Glowing Heart ❤️ ✅, Broken Heart, Dark Box

##### 🗣️ Group B: `speak_repeat` Mechanics (Questions 9 to 14)
9. **Q9:** Say out loud clearly: *"GOD GIVES ME COURAGE!"*
   - *Target Word:* `God Gives Me Courage`
10. **Q10:** Say out loud: *"DAVID TRUSTED GOD!"*
    - *Target Word:* `David Trusted God`
11. **Q11:** Say out loud: *"THE LORD IS MY SHEPHERD!"*
    - *Target Word:* `The Lord Is My Shepherd`
12. **Q12:** Say out loud: *"GOD IS MY HELPER!"*
    - *Target Word:* `God Is My Helper`
13. **Q13:** Say out loud: *"BE BRAVE AND KIND!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Be Brave And Kind`
14. **Q14:** Say out loud: *"AMEN!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Amen`

##### 🧩 Group C: `matching` Mechanics (Questions 15 to 20)
15. **Q15:** Match Bible heroes to their stories!
    - *Pair 1:* `David` ↔ `Courage & Sling 🪨`
    - *Pair 2:* `Noah` ↔ `Ark & Rainbow ⛵`
    - *Pair 3:* `Daniel` ↔ `Lions' Den 🦁`
16. **Q16:** Match story symbols!
    - *Pair 1:* `David` ↔ `Little Shepherd 🐑`
    - *Pair 2:* `Goliath` ↔ `Giant Sword ⚔️`
    - *Pair 3:* `God` ↔ `Shield of Faith 🛡️`
17. **Q17:** Match Bible lessons to actions!
    - *Pair 1:* `Courage` ↔ `Trusting God when scared`
    - *Pair 2:* `Love` ↔ `Helping a friend`
    - *Pair 3:* `Obedience` ↔ `Listening to parents`
18. **Q18:** Match Bible verses!
    - *Pair 1:* `The Lord is` ↔ `My Shepherd`
    - *Pair 2:* `I will fear` ↔ `No Evil`
    - *Pair 3:* `God is my` ↔ `Strength`
19. **Q19:** Match character qualities!
    - *Pair 1:* `David` ↔ `Brave`
    - *Pair 2:* `Goliath` ↔ `Proud`
    - *Pair 3:* `Saul` ↔ `King`
20. **Q20:** Match story cards!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sling` ↔ `5 Smooth Stones 🪨`
    - *Pair 2:* `Sheep` ↔ `Green Pastures 🌿`
    - *Pair 3:* `Crown` ↔ `King David 👑`
