KiddoQuest CBC Curriculum PP1 Mathematics
Level
Pre-Primary One (PP1)
Overall Goal
Enable learners to build a strong foundation in early mathematics by developing number sense, counting, simple operations, patterns, shapes, measurement, money, time, and problem-solving through interactive activities aligned with the CBC curriculum.

Content Strategy
Every mission should contain:
8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
5 randomly selected questions per play session.
Bright 2D illustrations.
Short mission video.
Audio narration using the question prompt.
Multiple question types where appropriate (multiple choice, matching, sequencing, drag & sort, count objects, patterns, etc.).
Increasing difficulty across missions so learners progress naturally without feeling overwhelmed.
