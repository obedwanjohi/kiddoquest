# 📘 Play Group English: Batch 8 (Missions 71 – 75)
## Writing Readiness & Pre-Tracing Safari
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 71: `PG-ENG-5.1.71` — Tracing Straight Lines
* **Theme:** Fine Motor Skills & Straight Line Tracing
* **Educational Objective:** Guide the finger/pencil along straight lines (Top to Bottom, Left to Right).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Straight line finger tracing and directional control.
* **Objects & Characters:** Giant Crayon, Dotted Trail, Falling Raindrop, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo holds a giant colorful crayon and traces a straight slide from top to bottom: *"Wheee! Straight lines go down, down, down!"*

---

### Mission 72: `PG-ENG-5.1.72` — Tracing Curved Lines
* **Theme:** Curve Control & Arc Motions
* **Educational Objective:** Trace smooth curved lines (Rainbow Arcs, Bouncy Waves).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Curved finger movement and smooth arc tracing.
* **Objects & Characters:** Colorful Rainbow Arc, Ocean Wave, Flying Butterfly, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* A graceful butterfly flies over a rainbow arc! Leo follows the butterfly with his crayon, drawing smooth curved lines.

### Mission 73: `PG-ENG-5.1.73` — Tracing Zigzag Paths
* **Theme:** Direction Change & Diagonal Tracing
* **Educational Objective:** Trace sharp zigzag lines (Up-Down-Up-Down like lightning or mountain peaks).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Corner turning and diagonal finger tracing.
* **Objects & Characters:** Mountain Peak Path, Lightning Bolt, Hopping Kangaroo, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo hops along a jagged mountain trail with his kangaroo friend: *"Up and down, up and down! Zipping along the zigzag path!"*

---

### Mission 74: `PG-ENG-5.1.74` — Tracing Circle Shapes
* **Theme:** Circular Motor Motion & Round Tracing
* **Educational Objective:** Trace closed circular loops (Suns, Wheels, Balls).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Continuous round finger movement and closing a circular shape.
* **Objects & Characters:** Round Yellow Sun, Bouncy Ball, Rolling Wheel, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo traces all the way around a shiny round sun until the loop connects: *"Round and round we go to make a happy circle!"*
