# 📘 Play Group English: Batch 1 (Missions 1 – 10)
## Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 1: `PG-ENG-1.1.1` — Touch the Ball
* **Theme:** Listening & Action Commands ("Touch...")
* **Educational Objective:** Learn to listen to simple verbal commands and identify common objects (Ball, Cat, Sun).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Receptive language (listening comprehension) and matching spoken words to visual objects.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Red Bouncy Ball ⚽, Leo the Lion Cub 🦁, Sleeping Cat 🐱, Smiling Sun ☀️.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Multiple Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo the Lion Cub is playing in the sunny meadow with a bright red bouncy ball. Leo bounces the ball and invites the child: *"Hi friend! Look at my red ball! Can you help me touch the ball?"*

---

### Mission 2: `PG-ENG-1.1.2` — Clap Your Hands
* **Theme:** Physical Actions & Body Commands
* **Educational Objective:** Understand physical movement verbs (Clap, Stomp, Wave).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Recognizing action verbs and connecting them to body movements.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Leo 🦁, Happy Clapping Hands 👏, Stomping Feet 🦶.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions** (3 Action Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo hears happy jungle music playing! He starts clapping his paws to the rhythm. Leo turns to the child and says: *"I feel so happy! When you're happy, clap your hands with me!"*

---

### Mission 3: `PG-ENG-1.1.3` — Wave Hello
* **Theme:** Social Action & Friendly Greetings
* **Educational Objective:** Learn the physical gesture of waving and saying "Hello".
* **What We Test (Skills):** Friendly greeting response and visual recognition of waving hands.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Leo 🦁, Waving Paw 👋, Friendly Safari Friends (Giraffe 🦒, Elephant 🐘).
* **Recommended Question Count:** **6 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 1 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo is driving his mini safari jeep and sees his friend Coco the Giraffe standing by the road. Leo waves his paw big and high: *"Hello Coco! Let me wave hello!"*

---

### Mission 4: `PG-ENG-1.1.4` — Point to the Sun
* **Theme:** Pointing Gestures & Sky Objects
* **Educational Objective:** Learn to use pointing gestures to identify bright objects in the sky (Sun, Cloud, Bird).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Directional pointing and sky vocabulary.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Bright Yellow Sun ☀️, Fluffy White Cloud ☁️, Flying Bird 🐦, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Pointing Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* It's a beautiful morning on the savannah! Leo shades his eyes and points up at the sky: *"Look up high! The sun is so bright and warm today! Can you point up to the sun?"*

---

### Mission 5: `PG-ENG-1.1.5` — Stand Up, Sit Down
* **Theme:** Opposite Physical Commands
* **Educational Objective:** Understand opposite action instructions (Stand Up vs. Sit Down).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Positional/action contrasts and dual verbal commands.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Chair/Stool 🪑, Standing Leo 🦁, Sitting Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Choice Contrasts, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo is playing a fun game of 'Simon Says' on the savannah grass. He jumps up: *"Stand up high!"* Then he sits down on his cozy mat: *"Sit down low!"* He invites the child to play along.

---

### Mission 6: `PG-ENG-1.1.6` — Open and Close
* **Theme:** Opposite Manipulation Actions
* **Educational Objective:** Understand the concept of "Open" and "Close" using everyday items.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Action concepts (Open Door/Box vs. Closed Door/Box).
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Treasure Chest 📦, Gift Box 🎁, Door 🚪, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Action Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo finds a mysterious surprise box wrapped with a big ribbon! He opens the lid to peek inside: *"Open the box! Wow! Now let's close it tight!"*

---

### Mission 7: `PG-ENG-1.2.7` — Greetings Safari: "Jambo!"
* **Theme:** Cultural & Friendly Safari Greetings
* **Educational Objective:** Learn the classic East African safari greeting "Jambo!" and its meaning ("Hello!").
* **What We Test (Skills):** Cultural greeting recognition and pronunciation of "Jambo!".
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Leo in Safari Hat 🤠🦁, Acacia Tree 🌳, Safari Badge 🏷️.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **6 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 1 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Welcome to the Safari Adventure! Leo puts on his ranger hat and greets every child arriving at the gate with a warm smile: *"Jambo! Jambo means Hello on Safari!"*

---

### Mission 8: `PG-ENG-1.2.8` — Saying "Good Morning"
* **Theme:** Morning Routine Greetings
* **Educational Objective:** Learn when and how to say "Good Morning" when the sun rises.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Time-of-day greeting association (Morning = Sun Rising / Rooster / Waking Up).
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Rising Sun 🌅, Crowing Rooster 🐓, Breakfast Bowl 🥣, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* The rooster crows *"Cock-a-doodle-doo!"* as the golden sun rises over the safari hills. Leo stretches his arms, rubs his eyes, and sings: *"Good Morning sun! Good morning friends!"*

---

### Mission 9: `PG-ENG-1.2.9` — Saying "Thank You"
* **Theme:** Politeness & Manners
* **Educational Objective:** Understand polite social interaction and saying "Thank You" when receiving a gift/help.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Social etiquette, identifying polite responses.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Gift Box 🎁, Juicy Red Apple 🍎, Grateful Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **6 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 1 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Coco the Giraffe picks a fresh, delicious apple from a tall branch and hands it to Leo. Leo smiles warmly and says: *"Thank you, Coco! Saying Thank You makes everyone happy!"*

---

### Mission 10: `PG-ENG-1.2.10` — Saying "Goodbye"
* **Theme:** Farewell Greetings
* **Educational Objective:** Learn the farewell phrase "Goodbye!" at the end of an activity or day.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Identifying farewell situations (waving goodbye, leaving for home).
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Sunset 🌇, Safari Jeep 🚙, Waving Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **6 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 1 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* The sun is setting behind the hills and the safari day is coming to an end. Leo hops into his jeep, waves to his animal friends, and says: *"Goodbye friends! See you next time!"*
