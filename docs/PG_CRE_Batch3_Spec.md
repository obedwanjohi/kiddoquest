# 📘 Play Group CRE: Batch 3 (Missions 21 – 30)
## Unit 3: Christian Values & Living Together
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 21: `PG-CRE-3.1.21` — Sharing Toys with Friends
* **Theme:** Generosity & Sharing
* **Educational Objective:** Learn that sharing toys makes God happy and brings joy to our friends.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Identifying sharing actions vs. keeping toys away.
* **Objects & Characters:** Shared Toy Ball, Teddy Bear, Smiling Friends, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo smiles and hands his favorite teddy bear to his friend: *"Sharing brings joy to everyone!"*
