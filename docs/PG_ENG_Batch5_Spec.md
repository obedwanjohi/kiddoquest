# 📘 Play Group English: Batch 5 (Missions 41 – 50)
## Animals, Objects & Vocabulary Safari
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 41: `PG-ENG-2.2.41` — Farm Animal Sounds
* **Theme:** Farm Animals & Onomatopoeia Sounds
* **Educational Objective:** Identify farm animals (Cow 🐄, Sheep 🐑, Duck 🦆) and match them to their sounds (Moo, Baa, Quack).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Animal recognition and sound matching.
* **Objects & Characters:** Cow 🐄, Sheep 🐑, Duck 🦆, Old MacDonald Farm Gate, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo visits a sunny farm! A friendly cow goes *"Moo!"*, a fluffy sheep goes *"Baa!"*, and a yellow duck splashes going *"Quack quack!"*.
