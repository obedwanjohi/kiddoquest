### Mission 11: `PG-MATH-1.2.11` — School Backpack Counter

* **Educational Objective:** Count colorful backpacks and pencils up to 4.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Counting items up to 4 in a school context.
* **Objects & Characters:** Blue Backpack, Red Pencil, Classroom Desk, Leo 🦁.
