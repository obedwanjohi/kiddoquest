# 📘 Play Group English: Expansion Batch 1 (Missions X.76 – X.87)
## Fun Theme Adventures & Fantasy Phonics
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

### Mission 76: `PG-ENG-X.76` — Dinosaur Phonics Adventure
* **Theme:** Prehistoric Dinosaur Phonics
* **Educational Objective:** Learn dinosaur-themed letter sounds (D for Dino, T for T-Rex, R for Roar).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Letter-sound association with prehistoric themes.
* **Objects & Characters:** Friendly Dino, T-Rex, Dinosaur Egg, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo puts on a safari helmet and travels back in time to meet friendly roaring dinosaurs: *"D is for Dino! ROAR!"*
