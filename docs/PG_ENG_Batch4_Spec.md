### Phonics Song Pattern (Letters U to Z):
> **U U U — /u/ /u/ /u/ — Umbrella, Umbrella**  
> **V V V — /v/ /v/ /v/ — Van, Van**  
> **W W W — /w/ /w/ /w/ — Watch, Watch**  
> **X X X — /ks/ /ks/ /ks/ — Box, Box**  
> **Y Y Y — /y/ /y/ /y/ — Yo-yo, Yo-yo**  
> **Z Z Z — /z/ /z/ /z/ — Zoo, Zoo**

### Mission 37: `PG-ENG-2.1.37` — My Body Parts (Head, Shoulders, Knees)
* **Theme:** Body Awareness & Movement Vocabulary
* **Educational Objective:** Learn names of primary body parts (Head, Shoulders, Knees, Toes).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Connecting body words to visual body locations.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Head 👤, Shoulders 🦾, Knees 🦵, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo plays the classic song *"Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes"*! He touches his head, taps his shoulders, and bends his knees, inviting kids to dance along.

---

### Mission 38: `PG-ENG-2.1.38` — My Five Senses (Eyes, Ears, Nose, Mouth)
* **Theme:** Senses & Facial Features
* **Educational Objective:** Identify facial features used for sensing (Eyes for seeing, Ears for hearing, Nose for smelling, Mouth for tasting).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Sense organ identification and action mapping (Eyes = See, Ears = Listen).
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Eyes 👁️, Ears 👂, Nose 👃, Mouth 👄, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo points to his eyes *"I see a red flower!"*, points to his ears *"I hear a bird!"*, and points to his nose *"I smell sweet apples!"*.

### Mission 40: `PG-ENG-2.1.40` — My Feelings (Happy, Sad, Excited)
* **Theme:** Emotional Intelligence & Expression Vocabulary
* **Educational Objective:** Recognize basic emotional expressions (Happy 😊, Sad 😢, Sleepy 😴).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Identifying facial expressions and emotional words.
* **Objects & Characters Used:** Happy Face 😊, Sad Face 😢, Sleepy Face 😴, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions** (3 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo makes different funny expressions in a mirror! He smiles big when happy *"Hooray!"*, makes a sad pouting face when dropping his ice cream, and yawns when sleepy.
