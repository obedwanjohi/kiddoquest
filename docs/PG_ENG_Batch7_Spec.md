# 📘 Play Group English: Batch 7 (Missions 61 – 70)
## Classic Storybooks & Moral Tales
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 61: `PG-ENG-4.1.61` — Story: The Three Little Pigs
* **Theme:** Story Comprehension & Material Vocabulary
* **Educational Objective:** Understand story sequence and material words (Straw, Sticks, Bricks).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Story element identification and material matching.
* **Objects & Characters:** Three Little Pigs, Straw House, Stick House, Brick House, Big Bad Wolf, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **8 Questions** (4 Choice, 2 Speak & Repeat, 2 Matching).
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Three pig brothers build their houses! The wolf blows down the straw and stick houses, but the sturdy brick house keeps them safe and warm.
