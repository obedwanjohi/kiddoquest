# 📘 Play Group CRE: Batch 5 (Missions 41 – 50)
## Unit 5: Old Testament Heroes & Moral Stories
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 41: `PG-CRE-5.1.41` — Noah and the Ark
* **Theme:** Faith, Obedience & God's Rainbow Promise
* **Educational Objective:** Understand that Noah obeyed God by building a wooden ark, bringing animals two by two, and seeing God's rainbow promise.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Animal pair matching (2 by 2) and rainbow symbol recognition.
* **Objects & Characters:** Wooden Ark, Animals Two by Two, Rainbow Arc, Noah, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Noah builds a giant wooden boat! Animals march aboard two by two before a colorful rainbow stretches across the sky as God's promise.
