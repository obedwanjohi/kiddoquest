# 📘 Play Group CRE: Batch 1 (Missions 1 – 10)
## Unit 1: God's Creation & Myself
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 1: `PG-CRE-1.1.1` — God Made the Sky & Sun
* **Theme:** Creation of Nature (Day & Night)
* **Educational Objective:** Understand that God created the bright sun, blue sky, and fluffy white clouds.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Identifying sky elements and thanking God for daytime.
* **Objects & Characters:** Bright Sun, Blue Sky, White Clouds, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo looks up at the golden morning sun shining in the clear blue sky: *"Thank You God for making the bright sun and beautiful sky!"*

---
