# 📘 Play Group Math: Batch 1 (Missions 1 – 10)
## Unit 1: Early Counting (Numbers 1, 2 & 3)
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 1: `PG-MATH-1.1.1` — Safari Apple Counter
* **Theme:** Counting 1, 2, 3 Red Apples
* **Educational Objective:** Count up to 3 juicy red apples hanging on safari trees and in baskets.
* **What We Test (Skills):** One-to-one counting, quantity identification (1, 2, 3).
* **Objects & Characters:** Red Apples, Apple Tree, Basket, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo walks through the safari orchard picking delicious red apples: *"Let's count: 1 apple, 2 apples, 3 apples! Yum!"*
