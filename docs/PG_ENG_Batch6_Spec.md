# 📘 Play Group English: Batch 6 (Missions 51 – 60)
## Rhyming Words & Classic Nursery Rhymes
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 51: `PG-ENG-3.1.51` — Rhyme Time: Cat & Hat
* **Theme:** Phonemic Awareness & Rhyming Words (-at family)
* **Educational Objective:** Recognize words that sound the same at the end (Cat, Hat, Mat).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Rhyming word identification and auditory rhyme matching.
* **Objects & Characters:** Cute Cat, Top Hat, Cozy Mat, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo plays a fun rhyming game with a cute cat wearing a tall red hat while sitting on a cozy mat: *"Cat! Hat! Mat! They sound the same at the end!"*
