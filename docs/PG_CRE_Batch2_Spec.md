# 📘 Play Group CRE: Batch 2 (Missions 11 – 20)
## Unit 2: The Life & Stories of Jesus
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 11: `PG-CRE-2.1.11` — Jesus Loves the Children
* **Theme:** Jesus' Love for Children
* **Educational Objective:** Learn that Jesus loves every child and welcomes them into His arms (*"Let the little children come to Me"*).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Recognizing Jesus' warmth and love for children.
* **Objects & Characters:** Jesus, Smiling Children, Open Arms, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Jesus sits under a shade tree smiling warmly, gathering little children into His arms with love: *"Jesus loves all the little children of the world!"*
