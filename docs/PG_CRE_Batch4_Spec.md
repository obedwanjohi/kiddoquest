# 📘 Play Group CRE: Batch 4 (Missions 31 – 40)
## Unit 4: The Church, Worship & Daily Prayer
### Mission Specifications & Movie Scriptwriter Brief

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

---

### Mission 31: `PG-CRE-4.1.31` — Church is God's House
* **Theme:** House of God & Worship Community
* **Educational Objective:** Learn that the church building is a holy place where God's family gathers to pray, sing, and worship.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Recognizing the church as God's house and showing reverence.
* **Objects & Characters:** Church Building, Cross, Welcoming Doors, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo walks happily up the stone steps into a sunlit church building: *"Welcome to God's House, a place of peace and worship!"*
