# 📘 Play Group English: Expansion Batch 2 (Missions X.88 – X.99)

This document serves as the **master blueprint** for both the interactive app CSV quizzes and the animated movie intro scriptwriter.

### Mission 88: `PG-ENG-X.88` — Animal Phonics Kingdom
* **Theme:** Wild & Domestic Animal Sound Review
* **Educational Objective:** Review animal sound-letter connections (C for Cat, D for Dog, L for Lion).
* **What We Test (Skills):** Phonics sound matching across animals.
* **Objects & Characters:** Lion, Dog, Cat, Elephant, Leo 🦁.
* **Recommended Question Count:** **7 Questions**.
* **🎬 Movie Scriptwriter Brief:**
  > *Story Concept:* Leo visits the animal kingdom where every animal steps forward to sing their phonics sound!
