### Mission 21: `PG-MATH-1.3.21` — Outer Space Rocket Counter
* **Theme:** Counting 1 to 6 Space Rockets
* **Educational Objective:** Count shiny space rockets blasting into space up to 6.
* **What We Test (Skills):** Space item counting up to 6.
* **Objects & Characters:** Space Rockets, Planets, Moon, Leo 🦁.
