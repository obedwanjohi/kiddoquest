### 🍎 MISSION 1: `PP1-ENG-1.1.1` — Letter A Phonics Safari (/a/)

#### STEP 1: Mission Metadata & Outcomes
- **Adventure World:** Phonics & Reading Safari (Strand 1.0)
- **Sub-Strand:** 1.1 Phonics Letter Sounds
- **Admin Title:** Letter A Phonics Safari (/a/)
- **Kid Display Title:** Sound of A says /a/! 🍎
- **Target Skill:** Recognize letter A/a, say the sound /a/, and identify words starting with /a/ (Apple, Ant, Alligator, Ax).
- **Session Rules:** Draw 5 random questions per session from the 20-question pool.

#### STEP 2: The 20-Question Bank Pool

##### 🅰️ Group A: `multiple_choice` Mechanics (Questions 1 to 8)
1. **Q1:** Which picture starts with the /a/ sound?
   - *Options:* Red Apple 🍎 ✅, Blue Ball ⚽, Yellow Cat 🐱
2. **Q2:** Listen to the sound: */a/ /a/*! Which animal starts with /a/?
   - *Options:* Alligator 🐊 ✅, Bear 🐻, Dog 🐶
3. **Q3:** Tap the letter **A**!
   - *Options:* `A` ✅, `B`, `C`
4. **Q4:** Tap the lowercase letter **a**!
   - *Options:* `a` ✅, `b`, `d`
5. **Q5:** Which tiny insect starts with /a/?
   - *Options:* Ant 🐜 ✅, Bee 🐝, Spider 🕷️
6. **Q6:** Which picture does NOT start with /a/?
   - *Options:* Dog 🐶 ✅, Apple 🍎, Ant 🐜
7. **Q7:** Tap the big capital **A**!
   - *Options:* `A` ✅, `S`, `T`
8. **Q8:** Find the red apple starting with /a/!
   - *Options:* Apple 🍎 ✅, Cup 🥛, Hat 🧢

##### 🗣️ Group B: `speak_repeat` Mechanics (Questions 9 to 14)
9. **Q9:** Say out loud: */a/ /a/ APPLE!*
   - *Target Word:* `/a/ /a/ Apple`
10. **Q10:** Say out loud: *"LETTER A!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Letter A`
11. **Q11:** Say out loud: */a/ /a/ ANT!*
    - *Target Word:* `/a/ /a/ Ant`
12. **Q12:** Say out loud: */a/ /a/ ALLIGATOR!*
    - *Target Word:* `/a/ /a/ Alligator`
13. **Q13:** Say out loud: *"A IS FOR APPLE!"*
    - *Target Word:* `A Is For Apple`
14. **Q14:** Say out loud: */a/ /a/ /a/!*
    - *Target Word:* `/a/ /a/ /a/`

##### 🧩 Group C: `matching` Mechanics (Questions 15 to 20)
15. **Q15:** Match Uppercase A to Lowercase a!
    - *Pair 1:* `A` ↔ `a`
    - *Pair 2:* `B` ↔ `b`
    - *Pair 3:* `C` ↔ `c`
16. **Q16:** Match pictures starting with /a/ to letter A!
    - *Pair 1:* `Apple 🍎` ↔ `Letter A`
    - *Pair 2:* `Ant 🐜` ↔ `Letter A`
    - *Pair 3:* `Alligator 🐊` ↔ `Letter A`
17. **Q17:** Match letter sounds!
    - *Pair 1:* `Letter A` ↔ `/a/ Sound`
    - *Pair 2:* `Letter B` ↔ `/b/ Sound`
    - *Pair 3:* `Letter C` ↔ `/c/ Sound`
18. **Q18:** Match words to pictures!
    - *Pair 1:* `Apple` ↔ `🍎`
    - *Pair 2:* `Ant` ↔ `🐜`
    - *Pair 3:* `Alligator` ↔ `🐊`
19. **Q19:** Match letter shapes!
    - *Pair 1:* `Capital A` ↔ `Big A`
    - *Pair 2:* `Small a` ↔ `Little a`
    - *Pair 3:* `Red A` ↔ `Red`
20. **Q20:** Match beginning sounds!
    - *Pair 1:* `/a/` ↔ `Apple`
    - *Pair 2:* `/b/` ↔ `Ball`
    - *Pair 3:* `/c/` ↔ `Cat`
