Content Strategy
Each mission should:
Teach one clear Christian principle or Bible lesson.
Use colourful 2D illustrations in familiar home, school, church, and community settings.
Include 8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
Present 5 random questions each play session.
Mix interaction types such as multiple choice, matching, sequencing, true/false, drag & sort, listen & choose, speak & repeat, and simple scenario-based questions.
Emphasize applying Christian values in everyday situations rather than memorizing facts alone.
