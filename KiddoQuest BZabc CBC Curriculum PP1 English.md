Content Strategy
Each mission should:
Focus on one specific English skill.
Use colourful 2D illustrations and audio support.
Include 8–15 questions in its Question Bank.
Present 5 random questions each play session.
Mix question types such as multiple choice, matching, sequencing, listen & choose, speak & repeat, fill in the blank, count objects, and complete patterns where appropriate.
Build vocabulary and reading confidence gradually through repetition in different contexts rather than memorisation alone.
