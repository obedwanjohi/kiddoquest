### ☀️ MISSION 1: `PG-CRE-1.1.1` — God Made the Sky & Sun

#### STEP 1: Mission Metadata & Outcomes
- **Adventure World:** God's Love & Creation Realm (Strand 1.0)
- **Sub-Strand:** 1.1 God Created Everything
- **Admin Title:** God Made the Sky & Sun
- **Kid Display Title:** God Made the Sky & Sun! ☀️
- **Target Skill:** Recognize that God created the sky, sun, moon, and stars, and express thankfulness to God.
- **Session Rules:** Draw 4 random questions per session from the 20-question pool.

#### STEP 2: The 20-Question Bank Pool

##### 🅰️ Group A: `multiple_choice` Mechanics (Questions 1 to 8)
1. **Q1:** Who created the bright yellow sun shining in the sky?
   - *Options:* God ✅, People, Nobody
2. **Q2:** Which beautiful creation in the sky did God make?
   - *Options:* Shining Sun ☀️ ✅, Plastic Toy 🧸, Wooden Box 📦
3. **Q3:** What shines at night in the sky that God made?
   - *Options:* Moon & Stars 🌙⭐ ✅, House, Car
4. **Q4:** Who made the blue sky above us?
   - *Options:* God ✅, A Carpenter, A Driver
5. **Q5:** Tap the picture showing God's creation in the sky!
   - *Options:* Sun & Clouds 🌤️ ✅, Table, Pencil
6. **Q6:** When we see the sun, who should we thank?
   - *Options:* Thank God 🙏 ✅, Thank Nobody, Cry
7. **Q7:** Who made the fluffy white clouds?
   - *Options:* God ✅, A Shoemaker, A Builder
8. **Q8:** Tap the bright sun that God created for us!
   - *Options:* Sun ☀️ ✅, Shoe 👟, Book 📚

##### 🗣️ Group B: `speak_repeat` Mechanics (Questions 9 to 14)
9. **Q9:** Say out loud clearly: *"GOD MADE THE SUN!"*
   - *Target Word:* `God Made The Sun`
10. **Q10:** Say out loud: *"THANK YOU GOD!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Thank You God`
11. **Q11:** Say out loud: *"GOD IS GOOD!"*
    - *Target Word:* `God Is Good`
12. **Q12:** Say out loud: *"GOD LOVES ME!"*
    - *Target Word:* `God Loves Me`
13. **Q13:** Say out loud: *"GOD MADE THE SKY!"*
    - *Target Word:* `God Made The Sky`
14. **Q14:** Say out loud: *"AMEN!"*
    - *Target Word:* `Amen`

##### 🧩 Group C: `matching` Mechanics (Questions 15 to 20)
15. **Q15:** Match God's creations in the sky to their names!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun ☀️` ↔ `Day Time`
    - *Pair 2:* `Moon 🌙` ↔ `Night Time`
    - *Pair 3:* `Stars ⭐` ↔ `Sparkle`
16. **Q16:** Match creations to thankfulness actions!
    - *Pair 1:* `God Made Sun` ↔ `Thank You God 🙏`
    - *Pair 2:* `God Made Me` ↔ `Happy Smile 😊`
    - *Pair 3:* `God Made Nature` ↔ `Care for Trees 🌳`
17. **Q17:** Match sky creations!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun` ↔ `Light`
    - *Pair 2:* `Rain` ↔ `Water`
    - *Pair 3:* `Clouds` ↔ `Sky`
18. **Q18:** Match time of day!
    - *Pair 1:* `Sun` ↔ `Morning`
    - *Pair 2:* `Moon` ↔ `Bedtime`
    - *Pair 3:* `Stars` ↔ `Night`
19. **Q19:** Match creation cards!
    - *Pair 1:* `God` ↔ `Creator`
    - *Pair 2:* `Sun` ↔ `Warm`
    - *Pair 3:* `Sky` ↔ `Blue`
20. **Q20:** Match prayers!
    - *Pair 1:* `Morning` ↔ `Thank You God for the Sun`
    - *Pair 2:* `Night` ↔ `Thank You God for the Moon`
    - *Pair 3:* `Meal` ↔ `Thank You God for Food`
