1. Subject Purpose
The Play Group Mathematics curriculum introduces children to basic mathematical thinking through play, observation, songs, stories, games, and interactive missions.
Children should learn mathematics by seeing, touching, counting, matching, comparing, and interacting with objects instead of memorizing numbers.
Every concept should be reinforced using multiple missions with different themes to build confidence through repetition without boredom.

2. Learning Outcomes
By the end of Play Group, a learner should be able to:
Count objects accurately.
Recognize numbers.
Match numbers to quantities.
Identify common shapes.
Recognize colours.
Compare sizes.
Sort objects into groups.
Complete simple patterns.
Understand positions and directions.
Develop logical thinking skills.

End Goal
A child who completes the Play Group Mathematics curriculum should confidently understand early number concepts, colours, shapes, sorting, patterns, comparisons, and simple logical reasoning, preparing them for the PP1 Mathematics curriculum.
This document becomes the foundation of your entire mathematics pathway. Every mission, question bank, video, image, and assessment you create later can be traced back to one of these strands and sub-strands, ensuring BZabc grows in a structured, curriculum-aligned way rather than as a collection of disconnected activities.
